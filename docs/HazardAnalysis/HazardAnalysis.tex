\section{Failure Mode and Effect Analysis}

The following Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA) table summarizes potential failure modes for the Lake system based on each component.
It also details potential causes and effects for each of the failures, along with a recommended action to mitigate
the potential failure. Components are listed based on their appearance above in Section 3. Beyond this, there are also
hazards listed for general communication failures that may take place in between nodes or components, as well as privacy failures.

  & Boundaries to ensure each device is allocated $>$0\% of the model, and re-balancing checks made throughout a session to verify boundary is not violated.

  & Ensure tolerances built into partition algorithm to keep models at (100-t)\% of device availability (t is specified threshold 
  that will be determined in testing).

  & A myriad of local failures could cause the device to not complete the inference. This could be hardware constraints or other device processes interfering.
  & Leader device is able to prevent failures from being shown to user and send retry request(s) to produce proper output.
  & SR2-1

  & Node receives incoherent tensors and cannot produce output to user.
  & User does not receive a response to their prompt and cannot use the system.
  & Communication failure (lost or corrupted data) between two nodes.
  & Place checks throughout the inference process for corrupted or lost tensors and restart inference from leader, temporarily removing the node where corruption/loss occurred from the network. Additionally, checksums 
  can be implemented to catch network corruption, with the system restarting if the checksums do not match.
  & SR3-1
  & H3-1 \\ \cline{2-7}
\multirow{-2}{\dimexpr0.2\textwidth\relax}{Tensor I/O}
  & Leader node fails to extract compressed tensors. 
  & User does not receive a response to their prompt and cannot use the system.
  & $i$ - Follower device fails to produce proper output or $ii$ - Leader cannot interpret output and run final inference to produce output.
  & Using the checks identified in H3-1, identify the node causing the failure and temporarily remove it from the network before restarting. If no problem is indicated from any follower node, restart
  inference and notify user of the issue.
  & SR3-2
  & H3-2 \\ \hline

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- H4
%
  % (General Communication: multirow label is in the third row below)
  & Communication between two nodes times out between devices or fails entirely.
  & Failure of system, loss of inference if midway through session.
  & Failure of communication protocol between nodes at hardware level (Thunderbolt, Wi-Fi, Ethernet).
  & Monitor average communication times between nodes and alert user to check connections if it exceeds a threshold. This can be measured through packet loss and throughput metrics. Restart inference if communication is lost and remove failed node.
  & SR4-1
  & H4-1 \\ \cline{2-7}
  & Device health status communication fails.
  & Leader node unaware if device fails, loss of system is possible if unknown device's performance is degraded.
  & Failure on behalf of leader node to send outbound requests, or failure of follower nodes to receive and respond to requests.
  & Monitor for devices not returning health checks, after multiple missed, remove device from inference network until health response is received by leader. In the case of leader node failure, the system's follower nodes will interpret a 
  lack of incoming health requests as leader failure and shut down since no further coordination can take place.
  & SR4-2
  & H4-2 \\ \cline{2-7}
\multirow{-3}{\dimexpr0.2\textwidth\relax}{General Communication}
  & Communication becomes noticeably slower for user compared to expected performance.
  & Users leaving the system and opting for another provider.
  & Inefficiency in between nodes or complete network slowness. Model is split up across too many devices causing too much communication overhead for each prompt.
  & Simplification of network. If inference is taking too long to complete based on historical system data, remove least capable devices on the network to reduce communication overhead.
  & S4-3
  & H4-3 \\ \hline

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- H5

  % (Privacy: multirow label is in the second row below)
  & Untrusted node on the device network.
  & Allows unintended access to the system and potential loss of data to bad actors. Reduces user trust in the system and may lead to loss of users.
  & Lack of initial authorization of each node or operation of the system on an untrusted network where unknown devices can join. 
  & Ensure that system is only operated on limited networks where all potential devices are known. Alternatively, the leader node must gain approval from the user before adding an unknown device to the network.
  & SR5-1
  & H5-1 \\ \cline{2-7}
\multirow{-2}{\dimexpr0.2\textwidth\relax}{Privacy}
  & Data leakage from a node leading to untrusted access or data leakage.
  & Untrusted users who have access to any of the participating nodes can see and steal data. Reduces user trust in the system and could lead to degraded or failed performance.
  & Lack of initial authentication and data protection measures in the system. 
  & Ensure that any devices in the network are explicitly approved by the user, so any potential data on intermediary nodes will not be tampered with.
  & SR5-2
  & H5-2 \\ \hline
